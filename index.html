Unera Stablecoin design system

The language of the Stablecoin Portal, where people issue and redeem hUSD and check the reserve behind it. It shares the Unera spine and reads more like an audited custody dashboard than a consumer app.

Shared for portfolio review. This is Unera's product, which is designed and not yet launched. Designed by Minh Nguyen as Lead Product Designer. Please don't reuse it. This public copy uses Hanken Grotesk in place of the licensed brand font, and sample figures replace real ones.

How it decides

These are the four rules the tokens and components follow.

  • Proof is a colour

    Verified Teal means on-chain proof, and nothing else uses it. Money-green means money moved. People learn quickly not to mix the two up.

  • One signature accent

    Signal Yellow marks the portal. It sits only on dark surfaces, where it passes contrast.

  • Every number has its state

    Pending, settling, completed and failed each have a tone, a label and a step on the timeline. A person always knows where their money is.

  • Restraint over reassurance

    There are no marketing adjectives on product screens. The data does the reassuring.

What the v2.1 audit found

I checked tokens, contrast, docs and rendering with scripts written with AI help (Cursor and Claude), then compared before and after screenshots.

The component cards needed _ds_bundle.js, which the export didn't include. Outside Claude Design all four component cards were blank4 cards
--text-tertiary and --shadow-popover were used by NotificationCenter and QuoteCard but never defined, so they always fell back to hard-coded values2 tokens
Money-down and error text (#ab5770) was 4.46:1 on the page grey, just under AA6 pairs
--brand-deep-blue is #1d1d1f (near-black) here and #173d47 in Huma and Admin. Same name, different colour across the family1
Hard-coded hex values that already exist as tokens150 uses
38 tokens are never used, including the --space-* scale38

What changed

  • Bundle rebuilt. _ds_bundle.js is now built from every .jsx under components/ with tools/bundle.mjs. All 4 cards and both UI kits render.
  • Missing tokens defined. --text-tertiary is an alias of --text-secondary, and --shadow-popover of --shadow-menu.
  • Money-down and error text changed to #a5536b (4.76:1 on the page grey). --brand-red stays for fills.
  • Colours point at tokens. 71 hex values in the portal and UI kits became var(--token) with the same value. Screenshots match v2.0, apart from animated particles.
  • Third-party colours moved to tokens/third-party.css. WalletPill uses --brand-ext-ethereum.

The Unera family

Three systems share one spine: dark institutional chrome, one grotesk and fixed money colours. The chrome is Deep Blue in Huma and Admin and near-black in the Stablecoin Portal. Each system then adapts the spine to its own users.
